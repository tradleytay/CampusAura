New to CampusAura? Start hereA quick guide

There’s no setup you have to finish before exploring. Try any of these when you’re ready:

  • Browse places to stay and save ones you want to compare.
  • Explore campus events. “Event booking” means telling the organizer you plan to attend.
  • Browse student items or manage items you’re selling.
  • Student ID verification is optional; your account works without it.
